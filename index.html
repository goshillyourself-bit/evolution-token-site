The lifetime idea

Reward the first step.

The proposed concept is to recognize eligible early buyers over the life of the project. The exact formula, funding source, payout asset, snapshot rules, and legal terms must be published before any rewards can be promised.

What “lifetime” should mean

For trust, the live program should clearly explain:

  • Whether the rate applies to token supply, a reward pool, or trading fees
  • How wallet ownership and first purchase are verified
  • Claim frequency, network fees, exclusions, and anti-abuse rules
  • Whether the program can change and how changes are announced
